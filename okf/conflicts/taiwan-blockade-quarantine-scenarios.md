## Summary

Strategic defense analysts differentiate between two distinct coercive maritime isolation scenarios for Taiwan: a full kinetic military blockade and a law-enforcement-led maritime quarantine. In a kinetic blockade, the PLA Navy, Air Force, and Rocket Force enforce closed exclusion zones around Taiwan's major commercial ports (Kaohsiung, Keelung, Taichung, and Hualien), laying naval minefields, interdicting merchant shipping, and severing subsea communication cables. In a quarantine scenario, Beijing utilizes the China Coast Guard (CCG) and Maritime Militia to declare a customs inspection zone in the Taiwan Strait, stopping, searching, and turning back select commercial cargo vessels under the pretext of domestic sovereignty. Taiwan is acutely vulnerable to maritime isolation because it imports roughly 97 percent of its total energy, with domestic liquefied natural gas (LNG) reserves typically limited to approximately 10 to 14 days of consumption.

## Developments

### 2026-06-25

The Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS) ChinaPower Project released a report titled "How a Chinese Quarantine of Taiwan Could Unfold," demonstrating how Beijing could bypass a formal act of war by employing Coast Guard law-enforcement cutters to impose economic paralysis.

### 2026-04-18

Rhodium Group published an updated economic assessment projecting that a complete cross-strait blockade would disrupt over 2.5 trillion dollars in global economic activity annually due to halted shipping lanes and severed high-tech manufacturing supply chains.

### 2026-02-09

The US Naval War College China Maritime Studies Institute (CMSI) analyzed PLA Navy and CCG joint communication interoperability, highlighting coordinated deep-water interception capabilities along Taiwan's eastern approaches.
