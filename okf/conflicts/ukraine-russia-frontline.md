## Summary

The Ukraine-Russia war remains a high-intensity conflict with pressure concentrated along eastern and southern frontlines, while both sides also rely heavily on long-range drones and missiles. The bundle should treat frontline claims cautiously because tactical changes are frequent and often contested.

## Developments

### 2026-08-06

Recent reporting describes Russia seeking momentum while Ukrainian forces continue counterattacks and long-range strikes against Russian oil and logistics targets. Other recent reporting says Russian aerial attacks on Ukrainian cities have intensified, keeping air defense supply a central issue for Ukraine and its partners.
