## Summary

The Ukraine-Russia conflict continues to escalate, with Ukraine conducting drone strikes against Russian oil facilities, refineries, and logistics centers, exacerbating Russia's fuel crisis. Ukrainian forces have targeted locations deep inside Russia, including oil-loading terminals and refineries in the Rostov region, Ufa, Stavropol, and Tver. The conflict has led to a widespread fuel crisis in Russia, with gasoline shortages and rationing in multiple regions.

## Developments

### 2026-08-09

Recent Ukrainian drone strikes have targeted Russian oil facilities in Tatarstan, including the Yelabuga drone factory and the Tatneft oil refinery, as well as industrial facilities in other regions, causing damage and fires. Additionally, Ukrainian drones have struck oil tankers in the Sea of Azov, setting them ablaze, and hit fuel infrastructure deep inside Russia, including an oil-loading terminal in the Rostov region and a facility in Ufa.

### 2026-08-07

Recent Ukrainian drone strikes have targeted Russian oil facilities, including those in the Tver and Stavropol regions, as well as oil tankers in the Sea of Azov, exacerbating Russia's fuel crisis. Ukrainian forces have also hit fuel infrastructure deep inside Russia, including an oil-loading terminal in the Rostov region and a facility in Ufa, demonstrating their ability to conduct strikes far from the frontline.

### 2026-08-06

Recent reporting describes Russia seeking momentum while Ukrainian forces continue counterattacks and long-range strikes against Russian oil and logistics targets. Other recent reporting says Russian aerial attacks on Ukrainian cities have intensified, keeping air defense supply a central issue for Ukraine and its partners.
