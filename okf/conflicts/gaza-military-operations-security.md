## Summary

Military operations in the Gaza Strip have transitioned through multiple high-intensity urban combat and targeted counter-insurgency phases following the October 7, 2023 Hamas attacks. The Israel Defense Forces (IDF) have conducted extensive division-level ground maneuvers across northern Gaza, Gaza City, Khan Younis, and the southern border city of Rafah. Operational focus has centered on neutralizing Hamas subterranean tunnel infrastructure ("the Metro"), targeting senior commander networks, and establishing permanent fortified security corridors: the Netzarim Corridor (dividing northern and southern Gaza) and the Philadelphi Corridor (the 14-kilometer border strip between Gaza and Egypt). IDF operational control over large portions of the territory remains contested by decentralized Hamas insurgent cells employing ambushes, improvised explosive devices (IEDs), and short-range anti-tank rocket fire.

## Developments

### 2026-08-12

IDF operational communiqués and Reuters reporting confirmed ongoing targeted brigade-level reconnaissance raids and tunnel demolition operations in central and southern Gaza, with Israeli forces maintaining perimeter security along the Philadelphi Corridor.

### 2026-05-30

The Institute for the Study of War (ISW) and the Critical Threats Project (CTP) published an operational analysis detailing Hamas tactical adaptation toward small-cell insurgent ambushes following the degradation of its formal battalion structures.

### 2026-02-14

IDF engineering corps completed specialized mapping and neutralization operations along dozens of cross-border and intra-city tunnel shafts discovered in eastern Rafah and Khan Younis.

## Key Actors

- Israel Defense Forces (IDF Southern Command)
- Izz ad-Din al-Qassam Brigades (Hamas Armed Wing)
- Palestinian Islamic Jihad (Al-Quds Brigades)
- Egyptian Border Guard Forces
