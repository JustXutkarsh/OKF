## Summary

U.S.-China tariff policy remains anchored in Section 301 actions tied to technology transfer, intellectual property, and broader trade enforcement disputes. The live policy surface is not only headline tariff rates, but also exclusion extensions, review processes, and new investigations that can alter compliance costs for importers.

## Developments

### 2026-08-06

USTR materials show the China Section 301 framework is still active, with exclusions and four-year review processes continuing into 2026. Separate 2026 Section 301 investigations on structural excess capacity and forced-labor-related trade practices include China among the covered economies, adding adjacent tariff risk beyond the original China technology-transfer case.

## Key Actors

- Office of the United States Trade Representative
- U.S. importers and manufacturers
- Government of China
- Customs and trade compliance teams
