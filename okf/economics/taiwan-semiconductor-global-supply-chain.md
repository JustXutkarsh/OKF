## Summary

Taiwan occupies a dominant, irreplaceable position in the global semiconductor ecosystem, manufacturing over 60 percent of the world's total contract semiconductor chips and more than 90 percent of the world's most advanced logic chips (sub-5-nanometer and sub-3-nanometer nodes). Led by Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC), alongside UMC, MediaTek, and ASE Group, Taiwan's "silicon shield" is concentrated along the western corridor of the island in science parks in Hsinchu, Taichung, and Tainan. Because leading global technology corporations (including Apple, Nvidia, AMD, Qualcomm, and Intel) depend heavily on TSMC's advanced EUV lithography foundry capacity, any military conflict, naval blockade, or physical disruption in the Taiwan Strait would instantaneously freeze global production of smartphones, servers, automotive electronics, and artificial intelligence computing accelerators.

## Developments

### 2026-07-12

The Semiconductor Industry Association (SIA) released its annual global market report, projecting worldwide semiconductor revenue to surpass 650 billion dollars, while emphasizing that single-source foundry concentration in Taiwan remains the single largest operational vulnerability in global electronics.

### 2026-05-18

TrendForce published updated foundry market share data confirming TSMC held over 62 percent of the worldwide pure-play foundry market, with its 3nm (N3) and 2nm (N2) node pipelines fully allocated across major global AI and high-performance computing customers.

### 2026-01-24

Boston Consulting Group (BCG) updated its semiconductor supply chain resilience study, estimating that a total cessation of Taiwanese semiconductor output would trigger cumulative global economic losses exceeding 5 trillion dollars within its first year.
