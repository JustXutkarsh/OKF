## Summary

U.S. semiconductor export controls restrict China's access to advanced computing chips, semiconductor manufacturing equipment, and related software or technology where officials identify military, AI, or supercomputing risks. The controls operate through the Export Administration Regulations, Entity List designations, foreign direct product rules, and licensing requirements rather than tariffs.

## Developments

### 2026-08-06

BIS materials show that the control regime has expanded from the 2022 advanced computing and semiconductor manufacturing rules into later restrictions on semiconductor equipment, high-bandwidth memory, software tools, and foreign-owned fabs in China. The policy remains closely linked to U.S. national security concerns over advanced-node semiconductor production.

## Key Actors

- U.S. Bureau of Industry and Security
- Department of Commerce
- Semiconductor equipment and chip firms
- Chinese semiconductor manufacturers
