## Summary

Peace and stability in the Taiwan Strait have been formally recognized by major Indo-Pacific powers and the Group of Seven (G7) as indispensable to global security and economic prosperity. Japan has significantly bolstered its defense posture along the southwestern Nansei Islands chain (including Yonaguni, Ishigaki, and Miyako islands, situated less than 110 kilometers from Taiwan's eastern coastline), deploying Type 12 surface-to-ship missile batteries and PAC-3 air defense units. The Philippines, under the Enhanced Defense Cooperation Agreement (EDCA) with the United States, has granted US rotational forces access to key northern Luzon military sites facing the Bashi Channel and the Luzon Strait. Multilateral communiqués from the US-Japan-South Korea trilateral, the Quad (US, Japan, Australia, India), and G7 Foreign Ministers consistently oppose any attempts to unilaterally alter the cross-strait status quo by force or coercion.

## Developments

### 2026-07-22

The Ministry of Defense of Japan published its 2026 Defense White Paper ("Defense of Japan"), identifying peace and stability across the Taiwan Strait as directly linked to Japan's own national security, and emphasizing the strengthening of standoff missile defenses across Okinawa Prefecture.

### 2026-05-11

The Department of Foreign Affairs of the Philippines and the US Department of Defense conducted combined coastal defense and maritime domain awareness maneuvers (Exercise Balikatan) in the northern Bashi Channel corridor.

### 2026-04-19

The G7 Foreign Ministers Meeting in Capri issued a joint communiqué reaffirming the fundamental importance of peace and stability across the Taiwan Strait as indispensable to international security and calling for peaceful resolution of cross-strait issues.
