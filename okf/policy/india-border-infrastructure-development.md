## Summary

The Government of India has executed an unprecedented, multi-billion-dollar strategic border infrastructure expansion program throughout the state of Arunachal Pradesh to ensure rapid military mobility, year-round logistics support to forward LAC posts, and civilian socio-economic integration. Spearheaded by the Border Roads Organisation (BRO) under Project Vartak and Project Arunank, flagship engineering feats include the bi-lane Sela Tunnel (excavated at an altitude above 13,000 feet to provide all-weather connectivity to the strategic Tawang sector, bypassing snow-bound Sela Pass) and the 500-meter Nechiphu Tunnel. In parallel, the Ministry of Home Affairs operates the Vibrant Villages Programme (VVP), funding comprehensive road connectivity, 4G telecommunications towers, clean energy grids, and eco-tourism across hundreds of designated frontier border settlements (such as Kibithoo, Tuting, and Zemithang) to stem rural out-migration and reinforce local civilian presence along the LAC. Long-term initiatives include the construction of the 1,748-kilometer Arunachal Frontier Highway (NH-913) running contiguous to the international boundary.

## Developments

### 2026-08-02

The Border Roads Organisation (BRO) reported the commissioning of three new reinforced permanent modular steel bridges capable of supporting 70-ton main battle tank transport along the Balipara-Charduar-Tawang (BCT) strategic highway corridor.

### 2026-05-19

The Ministry of Home Affairs conducted an inter-agency progress review of the Vibrant Villages Programme in Itanagar, noting the completion of 120 all-weather road links connecting remote frontier hamlets in Upper Subansiri and Dibang Valley to national highways.

### 2024-03-09

Prime Minister Narendra Modi formally inaugurated the Sela Tunnel in Arunachal Pradesh, establishing the world's longest bi-lane tunnel above 13,000 feet and significantly reducing military transit times between Tezpur and Tawang.
