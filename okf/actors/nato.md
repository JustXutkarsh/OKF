## Summary

NATO is a defensive military alliance whose current posture is shaped heavily by Russia's war against Ukraine, collective defense commitments, and higher allied defense spending. In this bundle, NATO matters mainly as a security actor connected to Ukraine support, European deterrence, and military-industrial capacity.

## Developments

### 2026-08-06

NATO's 2026 materials describe continued adaptation of deterrence and defense, including stronger eastern-flank posture and expanded Ukraine support. At the 2026 Ankara Summit, allies pledged EUR 70 billion in military equipment, assistance, and training for Ukraine in 2026 and committed to sustaining at least an equivalent level in 2027.

## Key Actors

- NATO member states
- NATO Secretary General
- Ukraine
- Russia
