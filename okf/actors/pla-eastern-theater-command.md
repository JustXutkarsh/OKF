## Summary

The People's Liberation Army Eastern Theater Command (PLA ETC), headquartered in Nanjing, is China's primary operational joint command designated for operations directed against Taiwan, the Taiwan Strait, and the East China Sea. ETC exercises operational authority over three group armies (71st, 72nd, and 73rd Group Armies stationed across Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and Fujian provinces), the PLA Navy Eastern Theater Navy (formerly the East Sea Fleet headquartered in Ningbo), the PLA Air Force Eastern Theater Air Force, and coordinated units of the PLA Rocket Force (PLARF). ETC doctrine prioritizes anti-access/area-denial (A2/AD) capabilities, precision multi-axis missile strikes, rapid amphibious assault embarkation, electromagnetic suppression, and naval blockade cordons.

## Developments

### 2026-07-30

The US Department of Defense Annual Report to Congress on Military and Security Developments Involving the People's Republic of China detailed ETC's deployment of Type 055 guided-missile cruisers, Type 075 amphibious assault ships, and DF-17 hypersonic glide vehicle brigades.

### 2026-05-16

PLA Daily and Global Times published reports highlighting ETC joint interoperability drills integrating satellite reconnaissance, electronic warfare jamming pods, and long-range rocket artillery (PHL-191) stationed along the Fujian coastline.

### 2026-02-12

ETC naval aviation regiments conducted nighttime anti-submarine warfare (ASW) and maritime strike sorties in the Bashi Channel and Miyako Strait, demonstrating deep sea-lane interdiction capabilities.
